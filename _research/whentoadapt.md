title: >
  When to Adapt: Multi-Signal Domain Shift Detection for Efficient Training-Free Adaptation in Open-Vocabulary Segmentation
description: >
  We propose a multi-signal domain shift detector for training-free continual test-time adaptation in open-vocabulary segmentation.
  By monitoring visual change, adapter mismatch, and semantic drift, our method triggers adaptation only when needed,
  reducing adaptations by two orders of magnitude while maintaining segmentation accuracy on robotic hardware.
